Bloom Sort – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Bloom Sort (the "App") uses, why, and the choices you have. The App is published by Nebulor Games ("we", "us").

1. Information we do not collect

We do not ask for, collect or store your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. The App has no accounts and no sign-in.

2. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (unlocked levels, stars, hints, and sound and music settings) is saved only on your device. It is never sent to us. Uninstalling the App or clearing its data deletes it.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC: a banner at the bottom of the screen, full-screen ads between games, and optional rewarded videos (for extra hints or moves) that you choose to watch. To deliver, measure and protect these ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share with Google:

DataPurpose
Device identifiers, including the Android Advertising IDAdvertising, measurement, fraud prevention
Approximate location derived from your IP addressShowing ads relevant to your region, fraud prevention
App interactions with ads (views, taps), device and app information, crash and performance dataAdvertising, analytics, diagnostics

This data is processed by Google under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Data is encrypted in transit.

4. Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions where required): the App shows Google's consent form before personalised ads are used. You can change your choice at any time from Garden Path (level menu) → Privacy & ad settings.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Delete your data: uninstall the App to delete all game data on your device. For data held by Google, see Google's privacy controls at myaccount.google.com.

5. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes take effect when posted on this page, with a new "Last updated" date.
